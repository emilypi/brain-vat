## PDF.js assets

The build copies PDF.js CMaps, fonts, ICC profiles and WebAssembly assets from the
installed `pdfjs-dist` package. Their bundled license notices are retained beside
the assets. PDF.js itself is distributed under Apache-2.0.

Other dependency licenses remain available in their installed packages.
